# Code of conduct

TripWeave follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.
Contributors should communicate respectfully, accept constructive feedback,
focus on the project, and avoid publishing another person's private data.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately through the repository's
Security tab. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community.

The complete Contributor Covenant 2.1 text is available at:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
