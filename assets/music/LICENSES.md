# TripWeave default music catalog

TripWeave stores metadata only. Recordings are fetched on demand from the
`freepd.com` collection in the SoundSafari CC0-1.0 Music archive and are not
committed to this repository or bundled into client packages.

- Archive: https://github.com/SoundSafari/CC0-1.0-Music
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Original collection: https://freepd.com/
